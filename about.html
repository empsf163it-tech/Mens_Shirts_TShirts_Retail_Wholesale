Our Story & Philosophy

CONTEMPORARY MENSWEAR, CLEARLY PRESENTED.

A modern menswear label combining fine fabric sourcing, precision garment engineering, and a streamlined retail & wholesale experience.

The Founding Idea

PRODUCT FIRST. ALWAYS.

FORMEN was created to strip away retail noise and focus entirely on what makes a great garment: superior raw materials, clean tailoring, durable stitching, and intuitive body sizing.

Whether serving an individual customer looking for the perfect Oxford shirt or a retail buyer ordering 500 units for a department store chain, our interface and garments uphold the highest standard of clarity and consistency.

Quality Standards

OUR MANUFACTURING PILLARS.

Four uncompromising standards applied to every garment produced in our factories.

1. LONG-STAPLE COTTON

We source long-staple combed cotton and organic yarns to prevent pillage and ensure softness over years of wash cycles.

2. REINFORCED STITCHING

Double-needle flat-fell seams, 18-stitches-per-inch collar construction, and fused interlinings for shape retention.

3. PRE-SHRUNK FABRICS

All fabrics undergo bio-washing and Sanforization to guarantee zero shrinkage or torquing post-laundry.

4. ETHICAL PRODUCTION

Fair-wage, WRAP-certified manufacturing facilities with zero-discharge wastewater filtration systems.

5. RECYCLABLE PACKAGING

100% biodegradable polybags, unbleached kraft shipping boxes, and FSC-certified garment tags.

GENERAL & ORDER QUESTIONS.

For wholesale orders from our ready catalog, our standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) starts at 50 pieces per style across assorted size runs (S to 2XL). For custom private-label or OEM manufacturing, the MOQ is 200 pieces per colorway.
Standard retail orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours and arrive within 3–5 business days nationwide. Wholesale orders are shipped via express courier or freight based on volume; catalog bulk orders take 5–7 days, while custom production orders typically require 14–21 business days.
Yes! We offer wholesale sample packs containing fabric swatches for Oxford cotton, combed jersey, linen blends, and piqué along with sample size shirts. You can request a sample pack directly on our Wholesale page.
Absolutely. We offer complete OEM services including custom woven neck labels, screen-printed care instructions, custom hangtags, branded polybags, and custom button engraving for wholesale partners.
Our garments follow true-to-size Western standard sizing. Our Slim Fit is tapered through the waist, Regular Fit provides standard breathing room, Relaxed Fit is slightly loose, and Oversized features dropped shoulders. Refer to our Size Guide for exact inch/cm charts.
We prioritize natural, durable fibers: 100% Egyptian and Oxford Combed Cotton for shirts, 220–280 GSM Organic Heavyweight Jersey for T-shirts, Mercerized Cotton Piqué for polos, and 100% French Linen for summer collections.
Retail customers can return or exchange unworn items with tags attached within 15 days of receipt for a full refund or exchange. For wholesale orders, we perform strict QA before dispatch; damaged goods reported within 7 days will be replaced free of charge.
For retail orders, we accept all major Credit/Debit cards, UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD). For wholesale transactions, we support Direct Bank Wire (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), LC (Letter of Credit) for international orders, and credit terms for verified recurring business buyers.
