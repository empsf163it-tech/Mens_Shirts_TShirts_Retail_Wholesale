Measurement Guide

HOW TO MEASURE YOURSELF ACCURATELY.

Follow these 4 quick steps using a flexible tape measure to find your exact size.

1. NECK / COLLAR

Measure around the base of your neck, keeping one finger behind the tape for comfort room.

2. CHEST

Measure under your arms around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level across your back.

3. WAIST

Measure around your natural waistline where your trousers comfortably sit.

4. SLEEVE

Measure from the center back of your neck, across your shoulder point down to your wrist bone.

5. GARMENT LENGTH

Measure from the top high shoulder point straight down to your desired hemline.
